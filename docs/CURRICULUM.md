`order` 1-10 = `beginner`, 11-20 = `elementary`, 21-30 = `intermediate`.
`(legacy)` = lesson already exists in `docs/legacy-v5-lessons.json`: keep its id, its 4 item ids/targets/meanings, and add 2 new items
(and improve the writing). All other rows are new lessons.
