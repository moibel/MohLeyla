## Please check these first (the reviewers were least sure)

**Pronunciation column** (capitals mark the stressed syllable; ç = the "ich" sound, kh = the "ach" sound, ew = ü, ur = ö): Kaffee (KAF-fay), Entschuldigung, brauche, Tschüss, zwanzig/fünfzig/dreißig (-tsiç), sieben, eins, Restaurant (res-to-RAHNG), München, Hamburg (final k sound), Informatik, Fahrrad, blau/grau (BLAOW/GRAOW), Sofa.

**Grammar and wording**
- `Mir tut der Bauch weh` (body part with the article, not `mein`).
- `zum Frühstück`, `zum Mittagessen` introduced as set phrases before the dative.
- `Kann ich mit Karte zahlen?` and `Ich möchte einen Kaffee` used as ready phrases early.
- `Wie sieht dein Tag aus?` in lesson 12 (aussehen early).
- `Doch` as the answer to a negative question (lesson 20 fill-in-the-blank: right answer Doch, wrong options Nein / Gern).
- `Am liebsten mache ich Sport`, `Ich höre lieber Musik`.
- `Wir werden euch aus Berlin anrufen` (anrufen takes the accusative), `seit zwei Jahren`, `Vor einem Jahr`.
- Ihr-imperatives: `Trinkt viel Tee und schlaft gut!`, `Kinder, bleibt bitte im Bett!`
- Konjunktiv II politeness: `Ich hätte gern ...`, `Könnten Sie ...`.
- `die Cola` (the item uses `die`; some say `das`).
- Culture notes: card payment habits, returns and exchanges in shops, emergency numbers 112 and 110.

**Arabic and Indonesian explanations** (the columns beside each German line) should also be read by a native speaker of those languages, especially the descriptions of the ü, ö, ä and ch sounds.
