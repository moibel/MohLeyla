## Please check these first (the reviewers were least sure)

**Stress marks in the pronunciation column** (capitals mark the stressed syllable): kumusta (ku-mus-TA), ospital, botika, tapos (ta-POS), tumawad/tawad, tiyan, tubig, Sabado, dalawa/dalawang, wala, gamot, kape, kasingtaas, Palawan, Maynila (may-ni-LA), Baguio (ba-GI-o or BAG-yo?), upuan, hintuan, libangan, mayroon (may-RO-on), kaunti, kapatid, gulay, kayo (ka-YO), mura (mu-RA), kumakain, i-NOM, ulo, menu, loanwords such as basketball and tricycle.

**Natural phrasing**
- `Pwede po ba ang card?` and `Pwede po bang tumawad?` (shops and bargaining). Would you say `Tumatanggap po ba kayo ng card?`?
- `Pakibigay po ng menu` / `Pakibigay po ng bill` (vs `Pahingi po ng bill`).
- `Masaya akong makilala ka` as "nice to meet you" (vs `Ikinagagalak kitang makilala`).
- `Sampung taong gulang na ako`, `Walang anuman`, `Ingat po`, `Sige po, tingnan natin` (po from a doctor to a patient).
- `Dalawampung piso.` as a jeepney driver's reply (vs `Bente`). `Pwede ako sa Sabado` (vs `Free ako sa Sabado`).
- `Kumakanta kami sa videoke` vs `ng videoke`. `Nagko-commute ako papunta sa opisina`.
- `Magpagaling ka` as "get well soon" (vs `Gumaling ka agad`). `Kita tayo bukas` / `Magkita tayo bukas`.
- `Gusto ko pong magpa-book ng kuwarto` (vs `mag-book`). `Ilang gabi po kayo?` (too short?).
- `Ikalima ng Mayo ngayon` as the date form.

**Grammar and spelling decisions**
- `kasing-` words are written joined and unassimilated (kasingtaas). Should they be hyphenated or blended (kasintaas)?
- `kaysa sa iyon` vs `kaysa iyon`; `kaysa sa` vs `kaysa kay` before names.
- Spellings: kumusta/kamusta, pwede/puwede, kuwarto/kwarto, kasi/kase, baryo/barrio, istasyon.
- Completed forms `bumili` and `bumaba` appear a few lessons before the completed aspect is taught, as ready phrases.

**Facts stated in notes:** 911 as the national emergency number; `salamat` from Arabic via Malay; `kumusta` from Spanish "cómo está"; the look-alikes with Indonesian (lima, kanan, mahal, sakit, tulong, ko/mo ~ ku/mu).
